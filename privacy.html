NIGHTLIFT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

NIGHTLIFT is a private fitness tracker built for a small group of friends. It is a personal, non-commercial project. This policy explains what it stores and how it handles your data in plain terms.

What we store

When you use NIGHTLIFT, the app stores the data you enter: your workouts and reps, bodyweight and waist check-ins, food and drink entries, profile details (name, height, weight, body type, training goals, and equipment), and messages you send to other members of your crew.

This data is saved to a private Supabase database and cached in your own browser so the app works across your devices. It is visible only to members of your crew who have the shared access code.

Oura Ring data

If you choose to connect your Oura Ring, NIGHTLIFT requests read-only access to your daily readiness and sleep summaries through Oura's official API, and only after you grant permission on Oura's own consent screen. These scores are used to show you a training recommendation (push hard or back off) for the day.

Your Oura access token is stored only in your own browser on the device you connect from. It is never shared with other members, never sold, and never used for any purpose other than displaying your readiness and sleep in the app. You can disconnect Oura at any time from the app, which removes the token from your device. Oura data is accessed read-only; NIGHTLIFT never writes to or changes anything in your Oura account.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or third parties. We do not use it for marketing. There are no trackers or analytics beyond what is needed for the app to function.

Deleting your data

You can clear your data from within the app, and disconnecting Oura removes its token from your device. For full removal of your records from the database, contact the address below.
